Module III

The World and Its Spell

Core Question: Which of your beliefs are truly yours, and which were given to you by the "Herd"?

This session explores the invisible systems of control that guide our default thinking. We will audit conformity, herd behavior, and the power of language using the works of Friedrich Nietzsche, Solomon Asch, and George Orwell.
